About us

We translate big ideas into bite-sized momentum.

Summeries & Sound is a micro-publishing studio that curates the smartest non-fiction and the most actionable podcast episodes so you can learn faster than your to-do list grows.

Our mission

We’re obsessed with helping busy people keep pace with breakthrough thinking. Every summary is crafted to surface the model, the story, and the playbook so you can apply it within minutes.

  • Curate the most relevant titles and episodes.
  • Extract the repeatable frameworks.
  • Record crisp audio so you can listen on the move.

How we work

Each week we track new releases, reader requests, and founder/operator interviews. The editorial process blends research-grade note-taking, expert interviews, and narrative editing.
