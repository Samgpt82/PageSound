FAQs

Do you take book requests?

Yes. Submit titles via the form above or tag us on social. We prioritize high-impact non-fiction in productivity, leadership, and wellness.

Can teams get private feeds?

Absolutely. We create branded collections with transcripts and discussion prompts for internal enablement.

What gear do you use?

Audio is recorded on Shure SM7B mics, mixed in Logic, and mastered with Izotope. Summaries are managed in Notion + Readwise.
