Deep Work

Author: Cal Newport Release: January 2016 Summary length: 1,100 words

Deep Work is a guide to cultivating the rare ability to focus without distraction on cognitively demanding tasks. Newport argues that embracing depth rather than shallow multitasking is the key to producing better work in less time, while unlocking creative breakthroughs.

Key takeaways

  • Schedule stretches of focused time and protect them like immovable meetings.
  • Eliminate digital distractions and engineer a ritual that signals it’s time to go deep.
  • Prioritize high-value, meaningful work and ruthlessly minimize low-impact tasks.
