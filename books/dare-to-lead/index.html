Leadership

Dare to Lead

Author: Brené Brown Release: October 2018 Summary length: 950 words

Brené Brown argues that courageous leadership grows from vulnerability, empathy, and meaningful connection. By choosing courage over comfort, teams create cultures where hard conversations happen and new ideas are safe to explore.

Key takeaways

  • Vulnerability is not weakness—it is the birthplace of innovation and trust.
  • Clear is kind. Give direct feedback anchored in shared values.
  • Leaders go first in modeling empathy, curiosity, and accountability.
