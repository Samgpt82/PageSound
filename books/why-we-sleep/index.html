Why We Sleep

Author: Matthew Walker Release: October 2017 Summary length: 1,300 words

Neuroscientist Matthew Walker makes the case for sleep as the single most effective step we can take toward improved health, productivity, and longevity. Optimizing sleep unlocks learning, creativity, immune support, and emotional balance.

Key takeaways

  • Treat seven to nine hours as a non-negotiable upgrade to every system in your body.
  • Consistency matters: hold a steady sleep and wake time—even on weekends.
  • Create a wind-down routine and sleep environment that keeps light, temperature, and caffeine in check.
